1. **[h013-confirmed]** Breadth panic with tech soaring = "bear market in disguise." The entry regime hard block is doing its job: 3,400+ stocks are falling while a few dozen semiconductor names rip. Opening new positions into this tape is asking for mean-reversion pain. Cash is a position.

3. **[New signal]** When 兴森科技 delivers +30.93% in 3 days while the rest of the portfolio is red, it confirms that in bifurcated markets, only the purest theme exposure works. Adjacent-sector plays (电子化学品, 电池, 消费电子) give diversification comfort without real protection. Next time the regime allows entries, lean harder into the #1 theme.

4. **[New heuristic]** Today's -3.17% in 新宙邦 with elevated MA10 distance (9.2%) is a reminder that even great catalysts (宁德时代30万吨协议) can't prevent a pullback when the stock has run too far ahead of support. For existing positions, this is manageable; for new entries, it would have been deadly.
