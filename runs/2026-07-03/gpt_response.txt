3. **[confirming h027] MA-distance discipline remains critical**: 隆达股份 (+12.1% above MA5) had the perfect catalyst (燃气轮机+军工) and sector tailwind (航天装备 #3, 贵金属 #1), but the anti-chase rule correctly blocked entry. Chasing a +12% spike above MA5 is how momentum traders get trapped.
