3. **New: Entry timing in deteriorating tapes is lethal.** 冰轮环境 was bought yesterday at 42.85 when breadth was already deteriorating. Today it's -3.85%. The thesis is strong but the tape killed it. Lesson: even if a stock passes all individual filters, if the broader market regime is weakening, wait. The regime gate exists for exactly this reason.

4. **New: The -3% in 3 days rule catches bad entries fast.** 冰轮环境 went from entry to -3.85% in one day. V1 would have said "thesis still valid, hold." V2 says "cut it." This is the right behavior — the opportunity cost of holding a losing position in cash is a winning position elsewhere.

5. **Sector gravity observation**: The fact that only defense and banks are green while resources/consumer/tech broadly sell off confirms a classic risk-off rotation. When this pattern emerges, even strong stocks in weak sectors get dragged down. 通用设备 (冰轮环境's sector) is not in the top 30% of sectors today.
