1. **[NEW — signal]** When all 3 major indices are green but breadth is below 1:1, it's a mega-cap bifurcation. The index move is unreliable as a buy signal — it masks broad underlying weakness. Today: 科创50 +8.41% but 2888 stocks declined. This pattern has historically preceded mean-reversion in the hot sector. **Type:** signal, **Tags:** entry-filter, breadth, **Evidence:** supporting, **Mechanism:** Large-cap weight in indices means a narrow rally can lift the headline while most stocks suffer — breadth tells the true story.

2. **[NEW — observation]** The 长鑫科技 IPO catalyst (July 16 listing) is a "concept/theme" catalyst — it creates a halo effect across the entire semiconductor supply chain. These halo rallies typically last 3-5 days around the event and then fade post-listing. The best entry window is now (pre-IPO) but only if breadth supports it. **Type:** observation, **Tags:** sector, timing, catalyst, **Mechanism:** IPO of a major industry player triggers re-rating of peers as the market prices in the ecosystem value uplift.

3. **[NEW — rule confirmation]** Today confirms h013 (breadth is the truth) and h019 (cold sectors = no-buy zones). Even with the #1 sector surging +8.55%, the broad market weakness makes new entries dangerous. **Type:** observation, **Tags:** entry-filter, **Evidence:** supporting, **Related:** h013, h019.
