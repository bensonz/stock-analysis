## 7. Uncertainty Flags

- **I'm uncertain about the persistence of today's semiconductor strength.** The divergence between 科创50 (+3.19%) and 上证指数 (-0.46%) is extreme. This could be the start of a genuine sector rotation, or it could be a dead-cat bounce in semis while the broader market rolls over. The breadth data suggests the latter is more likely.
- **IV data is completely missing** — all proxies returned null. This is a blind spot. If IV were elevated (>50%), that would actually support selective entries during panic. If IV were low (<15%), it would argue even more strongly for staying out. Without this data, I default to the breadth signal.
- **The market opened higher (四大股指集体高开 per news) but then sold off broadly.** This "gap-up and fade" pattern is a classic distribution signal. The morning enthusiasm got sold into by institutions. This pattern typically argues for continued weakness.
