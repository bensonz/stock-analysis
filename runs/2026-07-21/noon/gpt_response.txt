## 6. LEARNINGS

1. **Post-crash bounce with weak breadth is a false signal.** Indices up +3-7% but more stocks down than up = index-level buying (国家队/机构), not broad recovery. Wait for breadth to cross above 1.5:1 before deploying. *Mechanism: 国家队 buys index heavyweights/ETFs which lifts indices, but underlying market internals remain weak until the deleveraging cycle completes.*

2. **IV Rank 100% creates a paradox for new entries.** Historically this is a great medium-term entry zone (fear = opportunity), but the V2 framework's buy gate correctly blocks entries until breadth confirms. Being "right but early" in a momentum system is still wrong. *Mechanism: Extreme IV means the market is pricing massive 2-way risk — entering without breadth confirmation is gambling on direction, not riding momentum.*

3. **凯莱英's MA structure is the cleanest in the pool.** dist_ma20 at +5.3% (still above MA20 after the crash) shows the medium-term trend survived the deleveraging. When breadth confirms, this should be the first entry. *Mechanism: Stocks that hold above MA20 through a sector-wide crash have relative strength that attracts institutional buyers first in the recovery.*

4. **The 融资 deleveraging signal is pervasive.** 18 of 28 enriched candidates show "deleveraging" on margin. This is a macro phenomenon, not stock-specific. The signal loses differentiation value when it's universal — it confirms we're in a deleveraging cycle rather than flagging individual stock risk. *Related: active_learnings — the broad deleveraging supports h077 (hard block preventing FOMO entries during panic).*

5. **Sector-first approach validated again.** 半导体 (+8.43%) and 电子化学品 (+8.43%) are today's leaders, and the top candidates (扬杰科技, 中石科技) are in these sectors. When breadth improves, the sector filter will naturally prioritize the right names. The framework works — we just need the gate to open.
