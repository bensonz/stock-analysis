## 6. Learnings

1. **[h028 confirmed]** Today's relative leaders are concentrated in semiconductors and electronics chemicals, while resources/agri/banking laggards are being aggressively de-risked. This pattern has now held through both the selloff and the bounce — tech leadership is structural.

2. **[New observation]** The 1.35:1 breadth today masks the quality of the rally: 590 stocks surged +7%+, only 29 hit limit-down. The breadth ratio is dragged down by 1398 stocks barely down (f0_2). A modified breadth metric weighting magnitude would show a much stronger tape. Hypothesis: when r7_10 + r10 > 500 and f10 < 30, the 1.5:1 gate could be relaxed.

3. **[New observation]** Post-panic recovery days create a tension in V2: sectors are hot but individual stocks are below MAs. Pure momentum says wait. But mean-reversion buyers will get the best entries. V2 must decide whether it's momentum-first or willing to buy the turn. Current answer: wait for confirmation (MA convergence).

4. **[New observation]** IV at 1.00 rank across all ETFs is historically a strong contrarian buy signal. But the last time this happened (July 17-20), it preceded today's +10.73% bounce. Panic IV is a regime indicator, not a timing tool.

5. **[h027 confirmed]** MA-distance discipline remains critical: 昭衍新药 at +19.5% above MA20 was correctly rejected despite appearing "strong" — this is exactly the kind of overextension that leads to mean-reversion losses.

6. **[h019 confirmed]** Bottom-list sectors (油气开采, 油服工程, 银行, 养殖) are hard no-buy zones. Multiple candidates (融捷股份, 金钼股份, 荣昌生物, 凯莱英) were correctly rejected purely on sector grounds.

## 7. Uncertainty Flags

- **Breadth gate calibration:** The 1.35 vs 1.50 call is marginal. With 121 limit-ups and all 3 major indices strongly green, I could justify a SMALL BUY. I'm erring conservative because the portfolio is already -4.38% and V1's biggest sin was forcing entries in borderline conditions.
- **MA-distance asymmetry:** All candidates are below MAs (not chasing risk). But being deeply below MAs is its own risk — these are stocks in downtrends bouncing. V2 doesn't have a clear rule for "too far BELOW MAs." Should it?
- **Catalyst strength vs technical damage:** 星宸科技's +583-650% earnings growth is the strongest catalyst in the pool. If I believe catalysts drive price, buying the dip in a hot sector makes sense. But V2's Rule 2 (buy strength, RPS 75-95%) was built for trending markets, not V-bottoms.
- **IV panic = opportunity or trap?** The system says IV at 1.00 historically marks buying opportunities. But buying into extreme volatility requires wider stops and smaller sizing — V2's -5% hard stop is particularly vulnerable in a +10%/-10% daily swing environment.
