2. **[NEW] The MA-distance filter is blocking exactly the right stocks.** 芯碁微装, 联瑞新材, 华锡有色, 兴森科技 — these are the most exciting names in the hottest sectors, and every single one is blocked by Rule 2b. These are the stocks V1 would have FOMO-chased and lost money on. V2's discipline keeps us out.

3. **[REINFORCEMENT] Sector persistence matters more than one-day sector rankings.** 通信设备 and 半导体 have been leading for months (通信设备 12-month +207%). When regime improves, these remain the priority hunting grounds. Don't get distracted by one-day spikes in secondary sectors like 油服工程.

5. **[OBSERVATION] Several enriched candidates show >20% dist_ma20 — these stocks have run too far too fast.** 联瑞新材 (+62.7%), 国瓷材料 (+46.2%), 芯碁微装 (+38.6%), 兴森科技 (+30.9%). These are not "dips to buy" — they are extended names that need weeks of consolidation before re-entry is safe.
