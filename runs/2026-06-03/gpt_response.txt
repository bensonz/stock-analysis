### 6. Learnings

1. **[New] Narrow rally trap:** All 4 major indices green but 63% of stocks are declining. This is not a healthy bull market — it's a concentrated tech rally while the broad market weakens. The breadth below 1:1 is a legitimate reason to stay in cash despite the index-level strength.

2. **[New] MA-distance filter eliminates 70%+ of candidates:** Of 29 enriched candidates, only ~10 pass the anti-chase filter. The ones eliminated are the exact type that caused V1 losses (芯碁微装 was previously bought at extended levels and got stopped out).

3. **[Confirming h019/h027]** Bottom-list sectors (食品饮料, 教育, 酒店餐饮) continue to be de-risked while tech hardware compounds. The sector-first rule is working.

4. **[New] 华锡有色锡 catalyst real but price has run too far:** The 锡/"算力金属" narrative is genuine (continuous limit-ups, 异常波动公告), but dist_ma5=12.6% means the entry window closed. Need to wait for a pullback toward MA10 (~¥57).

### 7. Uncertainty Flags

- **Index vs breadth divergence:** I'm uncertain how long this narrow rally can persist. Historically, when breadth is this weak while indices are strong, it often resolves with the indices correcting toward the breadth, not the other way around.
- **通信设备 +8.85% 5d:** This sector is on fire but almost every stock in it is extended. I'm uncertain whether there's any entry point at current levels.
- **华峰测控 询价转让:** The ¥388.98 reference price from the 询价转让 could act as a resistance/support level. The stock at ¥383.78 is slightly below this level — uncertain whether this is an opportunity or a warning.
