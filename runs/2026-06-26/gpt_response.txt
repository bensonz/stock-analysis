1. **[signal] Sector-first exit works:** 新宙邦's -7.61% drop today despite an intact catalyst proves that sector gravity overwhelms individual stock quality. The 电池 sector at -5.81% (bottom 5) dragged down even the strongest thesis. This validates the rule: "If a position's sector goes cold, SELL regardless of individual stock performance."

2. **[signal] Structural catalysts survive panic tapes:** 上海新阳 (+2.80%) and 路维光电 (+0.48% on 18× volume) both have structural semiconductor supply chain catalysts (Japan photoresist cutoff, mask localization). These are not sentiment-driven — they're physical supply constraints that don't care about one day of market panic. This is the key differentiator between "hold through panic" and "sell into panic."

3. **[rule] Stop proximity + cold sector = proactive exit:** When both the sector is cold AND the stop is <2% away, the compound risk of a gap-down breach is unacceptably high. The 03-03 扬杰科技 lesson (gapped to -8.37% past stop) applies directly here. Don't wait.

4. **[observation] 18× volume on flat day = accumulation:** 路维光电 traded 18.3× normal volume and gained just +0.48%. This volume-to-price-move ratio is an exceptionally strong accumulation signal. Institutional hands are building positions methodically, absorbing all sell pressure without driving price up. This is more bullish than a +10% day on normal volume.

5. **[heuristic] Market regime overrides all individual stock factors:** With breadth at 0.17:1 and all three major indices deeply red, even the cleanest setups (兴瑞科技 with dist_ma5=-0.3%, RPS120=94.5,创历史新高) must be skipped. The `hard_block` is correct and must be respected.
