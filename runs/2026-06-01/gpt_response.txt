## 6. Learnings

1. **The buy gate index criterion just proved its value.** Breadth was 2.24:1 (bullish), yet all three major indices were red. This is the classic "rotation, not rally" signal — big money selling large-cap tech while mid/small cyclicals catch bids. Opening longs into this tape means betting against institutional flow direction. The index criterion caught this correctly.

2. **Sector rotation is violent and decisive.** 科创50 went from all-time high on May 11 to -5% today. That's a 10%+ correction in 3 weeks. The coal sector (+5.6% today) is the mirror trade — Indonesia's export policy is a structural catalyst, not a one-day story. We should track whether coal leadership persists (if it does, we need coal-sector candidates in the pipeline).

3. **MA-distance discipline filters more than sector.** 6 candidates (芯碁微装, 联瑞新材, 国瓷材料, 卓易信息, 华锡有色, 兴森科技) were eliminated solely by Rule 2b despite strong RPS and fundamentals. These are the "visually appealing but dangerous" stocks. 联瑞新材 at dist_ma5=21.2% is a mean-reversion accident waiting to happen. Rule 2b is doing real work.

4. **VCP data remains scarce.** Only 山东赫达 (1/31 enriched) has VCP data. Without VCP signals on most candidates, we're missing the strongest backtested timing edge. This is a data pipeline gap worth flagging.

5. **V1's WATCH list stocks are confirming the rotation.** The missed_opportunity data shows prior WATCH recommendations (华峰测控, 伟测科技, 江丰电子, 芯源微, 思瑞浦, 兴森科技, 芯碁微装, 联瑞新材, 国瓷材料, 卓易信息) — many are semiconductor names that are now in freefall. V1 was right to WATCH them, but the "wait and see" approach on tech names would have led to losses. V2's sector-first approach would have blocked all these entries.
