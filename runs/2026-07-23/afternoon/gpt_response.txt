## 6. Learnings

1. **Hot sector ≠ buyable stocks.** 电网设备 is the #1 sector today but both candidates (华盛昌, 远东股份) are in deep corrections with -10% limit-downs. V2 correctly prevents buying broken stocks in hot sectors — but this also means sometimes the best sector has zero entries. Cash is the right answer.

2. **MA20 touch (dist_ma20 ≈ 0%) is the strongest pullback entry signal.** 凯莱英 at 0.0% from MA20 with RPS20=99.6% is the cleanest setup in the pool. This pattern (strong momentum stock pulling back to rising MA20) has the highest probability of continuation.

3. **科创50 -3.78% while 上证 +0.25% confirms persistent rotation.** The divergence between indices is a powerful regime signal. The market is voting with its feet: out of tech, into resources/defense. We must respect this — zero semiconductor buys, regardless of individual stock quality.

4. **V1's "WATCH everything" produced analysis paralysis.** V2's hard sector filter + MA extension filter eliminates ~80% of candidates immediately. The remaining pool is small but high-quality. This is the right approach — fewer, better decisions.
