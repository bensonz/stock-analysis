2. **Commodity rotation is violent and binary.** 能源金属 at +5.21% vs 半导体 at -4.54% in a single day. This is not sector rotation — it's sector regime change. Strategy pool is ~60% tech names. When the buy gate clears, the playbook shifts to commodities.

3. **科创50 IV at 84.56% is a panic signal.** Previous outlier spikes at this level (July 17-22) coincided with massive tech selling. The options market is pricing continued downside risk in tech. Any tech entry must wait for IV to normalize below 60%.

4. **博迁新材 illustrates the "hot sector, broken stock" trap.** #1 sector, RPS 99.4%, but price is -30.6% below MA20. Sector strength hasn't stopped the individual stock's downtrend. Sector gravity works both ways — hot sector helps but can't rescue a broken technical structure.
