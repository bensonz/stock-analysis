## 6. Learnings

### Today's Key Insights

1. **[signal] The entry_regime system works as designed.** Breadth 0.33:1 + 0/3 green indices + f10≥30 = hard block. V1 would have found clever reasons to buy "just a small position." V2 correctly stays cash. This discipline alone would have prevented many of V1's losses.

2. **[observation] K-shaped markets create false signals.** 元件 +4.42% on a day when 4,121 stocks are declining makes it tempting to cherry-pick. But individual stock selection cannot overcome systematic selling pressure. The 300236 setup is beautiful — and the correct decision today is still to skip it.

3. **[heuristic] MA-distance rule catches MANY candidates.** Of 25 enriched candidates reviewed, at least 12 fail the MA-distance check alone (dist_ma5>6% or dist_ma10>8% or dist_ma20>12%). This rule is doing heavy lifting — confirmed by [h021] and [h027] in active learnings.

4. **[observation] The best setups are pullbacks within uptrends, not breakouts.** 300236 (dist_ma5=-7.5%, near MA20 support) and 688037 (dist_ma5=-1.5%) are much cleaner than 300285 (dist_ma5=+11.6% at new high) or 002645 (dist_ma5=+15.1%). When the market reopens, prioritize pullback-to-support setups.

5. **[risk-flag] 华丰科技 688629 has a 61% lockup expiry on June 29.** This is a massive overhang. Even if the setup improves, this stock should be avoided until post-expiry clarity emerges.

6. **[mechanism] Hot sectors on panic days indicate institutional rotation, not retail panic.** Retail panic would hit everything uniformly. The fact that 电子化学品 and 元件 held +3-4% while 工业金属 dropped -4.5% suggests institutions are rotating INTO electronics, not fleeing equities entirely. This is bullish for electronics once the selling wave passes.
