## 6. Learnings

### New learnings from today:

1. **The hard block is working as designed.** V1 would have been tempted by 永和股份's refrigerant catalyst or 凯莱英's equity incentive. Both are good names, but buying into a 0.68:1 breadth tape with 81 limit-downs is a recipe for -5% stops triggering within 1-2 days. Cash is a position.

2. **Sector rotation clarity matters more than individual catalysts.** 星宸科技 has a 583-650% earnings growth catalyst — one of the strongest in the market. But its sector (电子/半导体) is getting annihilated. V1 would have bought this and lost money. V2 correctly recognizes sector gravity > stock catalyst.

3. **Extreme IV (100th percentile) across all ETF proxies is a regime-level signal.** When every index — 500, 科创50, 创业板 — is simultaneously at max IV rank, it means systemic stress, not just sector-specific vol. This is different from isolated IV spikes. New entries in this environment face elevated gap risk and wider-than-modeled drawdowns.

4. **Most enriched candidates are deeply below MAs — confirming a broad selloff, not isolated weakness.** Of 20 candidates, 15 have dist_ma20 < -15%. Only 凯莱英 (+5.3%) and 昭衍新药 (+19.5%) are above MA20. This tells us the selloff is broad and deep — it's not "stock-picking" time, it's "waiting" time.

5. **Margin signal divergence is informative.** 凯莱英 and 星宸科技 show `adding` (leveraged buyers accumulating on weakness), while most others show `deleveraging`. This divergence helps identify which names have institutional conviction vs. which are being abandoned. When the regime clears, the `adding` names should recover faster.

### Hypothesis updates:

- **h077 (confirmed)**: "The hard block is preventing FOMO entries." Today provides another strong data point — multiple candidates with excellent catalysts (永和制冷剂, 凯莱英股权激励, 星宸AI芯片) that would have been tempting buys in V1. The hard block correctly keeps us in cash.
- **h019 (confirmed)**: Bottom-list sectors as hard no-buy zones. 中石科技 (电子化学品Ⅱ, -7.55%) is a textbook example.

## 7. Uncertainty Flags

1. **I'm uncertain how long this rotation persists.** Energy/commodity strength could be a 2-week phenomenon or a multi-month regime change. The 制冷剂 structural bull case (supply caps + AI-driven fluorochemical demand) has legs, but oil/coal may be more tactical.

2. **I'm uncertain whether the extreme IV readings signal a near-term reversal.** IV Rank 1.0 can precede sharp V-bounces (panic capitulation) OR continued grinding selloffs. The framework's approach — wait for breadth to recover before re-entering — is prudent but may miss the initial bounce.

3. **I'm uncertain about 凯莱英's relative strength sustainability.** It's the only candidate holding above MA20 despite the CRO sector getting hammered (昭衍新药 2连跌停). This relative strength is bullish, but sector gravity could eventually pull it down.
