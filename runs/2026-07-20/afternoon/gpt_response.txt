## 6. Learnings

1. **[h077 confirmed — new evidence]** The hard block is preventing FOMO entries into a trap tape. 上证 +0.85% masks 267 limit-downs. V1 would have seen "index green" and bought. V2 correctly reads breadth and stays cash. Type: `rule` | Tags: `entry-filter` | Evidence: `supporting` | Mechanism: Index-level gains driven by heavyweight rotation hide severe underlying breadth deterioration. The up/down ratio and limit-down count are far more informative than headline index changes for momentum entry decisions.

2. **[New observation]** When IV Rank hits 1.0 across all ETFs simultaneously (as it does today), it signals systemic panic, not isolated sector fear. This is historically a bottoming signal — but the momentum framework correctly waits for confirmation (breadth recovery, sector stabilization) rather than trying to catch the knife. The trade-off: you may miss the exact bottom, but you avoid catching a falling knife that keeps falling. Type: `heuristic` | Tags: `entry-filter`, `timing` | Evidence: `supporting` | Related: h077.

3. **[New observation]** The enriched_candidates show that when a broad sell-off hits, even the highest-quality stocks show dist_ma5_pct of -10% to -30%. These are not "pullbacks to support" — they are liquidation events. The MA-distance rule (Rule 2b) was designed to prevent chasing extended stocks to the upside, but the same principle applies in reverse: stocks that have crashed -15%+ below their MAs in a matter of days have broken their trend structure and need time to rebuild bases. Type: `observation` | Tags: `entry-filter`, `timing` | Evidence: `supporting`.

4. **[New signal]** The sector rotation today (energy/resources up, tech/electronics down) is geopolitically driven (US-Iran) rather than fundamentally driven. Geopolitics-driven rotations tend to reverse quickly when headlines shift. The framework's requirement for sector persistence (not just one-day spikes) protects against chasing these false rotations. Type: `signal` | Tags: `sector` | Evidence: `observation`.

5. **[New observation]** 证监会 stabilization meeting + SOE buying (中国国新 ¥500B, 中国诚通 ¥10B) are policy responses that may create a short-term bounce. But policy-driven bounces in a momentum-down tape are unreliable for new positions — they create false signals. The framework correctly waits for market-driven breadth improvement, not policy headlines. Type: `observation` | Tags: `entry-filter`, `macro` | Evidence: `observation`.
