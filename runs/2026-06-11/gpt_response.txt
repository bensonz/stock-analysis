2. **[Validation of h013]** Strong breadth alone is not enough — but the inverse is also true: weak breadth should absolutely block new entries. Today's 0.28:1 breadth with hard block is exactly what the system was designed for.

3. **[Validation of stop_proximity rule]** 兴森科技 at 1.71% from stop in a panic tape is exactly the scenario the W10 post-mortem identified. Proactive exit prevents the gap-through-stop disaster.

4. **[Observation]** 上海新阳 and 路维光电 are showing genuine relative strength — both green today while the market bleeds. This is what "strong stocks in strong sectors" looks like even in a rotation. The 电子化学品 sector's momentum (4.98% last week) hasn't fully dissipated.
