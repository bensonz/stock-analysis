## 6. Learnings

1. **Sector rotation is real and must be respected.** Today's data shows a clear shift from tech (通信设备 -2.80%, 光伏设备 -2.41%, 元件 -2.16%) to agriculture/cyclicals (养殖业 +6.97%, 教育 +5.46%). When the candidate pool is tech-heavy and no candidate aligns with hot sectors, the right call is cash — not forcing an entry. [h028, h019]

2. **The -3% in 3 days rule works fast.** 恒铭达 was opened 06-30 at ¥77.88, is now ¥74.87 (-3.87%). In just 2 days, the thesis ("MA20下方低吸") was proven wrong. Cut fast, don't rationalize. This validates the V2 tightening from -10% to -5% and adding the 3-day rule. [new observation]

3. **Intraday reversals from 52-week highs on massive volume are danger signals.** 鹏辉能源 hit ¥92.32 (24-month high) then reversed to ¥75.47 low (~18% swing) on 6.5× volume. This is institutional distribution, not healthy pullback. Even though -1.04% PnL doesn't trigger Rule 5, the price action is extremely bearish. Tight leash required. [new signal]

4. **MA-distance discipline remains critical.** Of 34 enriched candidates, over 20 fail Rule 2b. Without this filter, V2 would have bought overextended stocks at the worst possible time — exactly what V1 did wrong. The rule is doing its job. [h021, h027 confirmed]

5. **近20家半导体企业7月1日起涨价** could provide a near-term support floor for semiconductor positions (路维光电, 上海新阳). But it does NOT override the sector rotation headwind for new entries. Use it to hold winners, not to chase new ones.

6. **Cash at 88% is not a problem in a rotating market.** The V1 lesson was that forcing entries to "stay invested" led to buying in cold sectors and losing money. Waiting for sector alignment to return is the disciplined move.
