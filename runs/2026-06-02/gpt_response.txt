### New Insight — Breadth > Indices
**Today proves that index-level green does not equal a healthy market.** All 3 major indices are positive, yet 70% of stocks declined. The buy gate's breadth filter (≥1.5:1 Up/Down) correctly blocked new positions despite the "green indices" illusion. This is a structural validation of Rule 1's gate.

### Supporting Evidence for Active Hypotheses
- **h019 (bottom-list sectors = hard no-buy)**: The bottom 5 sectors (影视院线, 广告营销, etc.) are all consumer/entertainment — confirming the rotation OUT of these into tech/resources. No reason to fight this.
- **h021 (MA-distance anti-chase rule)**: At least 8 candidates (芯碁微装, 联瑞新材, 国瓷材料, 兴森科技, 华锡有色, 光库科技, 欧科亿, 联瑞新材) would have been dangerous chases today. The MA-distance rule is silently protecting us from buying tops.
- **h027 (MA-distance discipline inside hot sectors)**: 华锡有色 is in the #4 hot sector (小金属) but dist_ma5=+12.6% — hot sector does not override chase risk. h027 holds.
- **h028 (leaders concentrated in communication equipment + tech hardware)**: Today's data reaffirms this — 通信设备 #1, 元件 #2.

## 7. Uncertainty Flags

1. **Breadth divergence magnitude is unusual.** 3,876 down vs 1,543 up while indices are green is a significant red flag. I'm uncertain whether this resolves as (a) a healthy rotation where large caps lead, or (b) the beginning of a broader selloff where the large caps are the last to fall. Given the 2.9万亿+ daily volume, my lean is (b) — when this much volume produces negative breadth, distribution is happening.

2. **Communication equipment sector's persistence.** +6.41% today is impressive, but this sector has already run +55% YTD. Is this a blow-off top or a continuation? The NVIDIA GTC catalyst is fresh, but the trade is extremely crowded.

3. **华丰科技's 解禁 overhang.** 61% of shares unlocking on June 29. The stock has great fundamentals but this is an existential risk. Even if breadth allowed entries, I wouldn't touch it.

4. **"算力金属" narrative durability.** Tin up 40% in 6 months is real, but the speed of the move suggests speculative froth. Supply constraints (Myanmar, Indonesia, Congo) are structural, but demand from AI is harder to quantify.
