If semiconductors bounce back tomorrow, we'll have missed the dip in our best names. My conviction: this is multi-day given the global nature of the sell-off (US, Korea, Japan all selling semis), but I'm not 100% certain.

2. **中国巨石 depth of pullback:** dist_ma20 at -26% is a big correction. The stock could be in a genuine downtrend, not just a pullback. The electronic fabric thesis is strong (5 rounds of price increases, AI-driven demand) but technical damage is real. I'm sizing at 5% rather than 7% to reflect this uncertainty.

3. **恒逸石化 debt risk:** Cash/short-debt ratio is 0.23, and interest-bearing debt is 67% of invested capital. In a high-rate environment, this is a real risk. The earnings explosion masks structural balance sheet weakness. 3% sizing is intentionally small.

4. **IV Rank data freshness:** All IV proxies show "is_live: false" — these are yesterday's closing IV levels. Today's market may have different IV characteristics. I'm treating the data as directionally correct but acknowledging it could be stale.

5. **Missing sector momentum data:** We only have 5-day sector performance (today's snapshot). Without 20-day sector momentum, I cannot confirm whether today's sector rankings represent a trend or a blip. This is a data gap that affects conviction on sector-first filtering.
