## 6. Learnings

**New learnings for today:**

1. **"Index green, breadth red" is the most dangerous tape for momentum entries.** Three major indices all positive, 科创50 +2.98%, but 2,908 stocks fell vs 2,471 rose. Large-cap tech is masking broad distribution. Buying into this would mean entering at the top of a narrowing rally. This pattern has appeared in recent weeks (ref: 6月1日 analysis noting "极致分化") and should be treated as a yellow-to-red flag.

2. **Glass fiber sector is in a confirmed structural super-cycle, not a short-term spike.** Web research confirms monthly price hikes since Oct 2025, AI server PCB demand 5x traditional servers, 日東纺 capacity bottleneck until 2027. This sector deserves a dedicated watchlist of 玻璃玻纤 stocks (国际复材, 中国巨石, 山东玻纤, 宏和科技, 中材科技). None of these are in our strategy pool — a gap to address.

3. **MA-distance rule is filtering exactly what it should.** On a day like today, 15+ candidates in the strategy pool fail dist_ma5 > 6% or dist_ma10 > 8%. These are the stocks that "look great" on momentum screens but would be chased entries. Rule 2b is protecting us from these.

4. **VCP data is scarce in our pool.** Only 1 of 27 enriched candidates (新宙邦) has VCP data, and it's only SETUP quality (contraction_ratio=0.48 > 0.4 threshold). The VCP scanner may need calibration or our pool is tilted toward extended names.

5. **The breadth gate is doing its job.** V1's biggest error was buying individual setups in weak tape. Today the system correctly says "no." 0.85:1 breadth is unambiguous.
