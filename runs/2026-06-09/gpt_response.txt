## 6. New Learnings

1. **The Nvidia-SK Hynix HBM deal is a textbook multi-quarter sector catalyst.** It lifts the entire semiconductor value chain simultaneously — equipment, materials, packaging, PCB, and chips. When the catalyst is this broad, buy the supply chain, not just the headline stock. The top 3 sectors today (电子化学品, 元件, 玻璃玻纤) are all downstream beneficiaries.

2. **Rule 2b (MA distance anti-chase) eliminated 8 of today's 28 enriched candidates — and all 8 were strong names that "looked" great.** This rule is doing exactly what it's designed to do: prevent buying into euphoric spikes. Yesterday's sell-off created fear, today's bounce created FOMO — the MA rule is the guardrail.

3. **0-risk stocks are clustering in today's hot sectors** (上海新阳, 路维光电, 恒铭达 all 0 risks). This contradicts V1's assumption that "risky" stocks outperform — today the cleanest setups are also in the best position. Risk count should be treated as neutral, not as a contrarian signal.

4. **新宙邦's 宁德时代协议 (June 7) is a genuine event-driven catalyst** that can potentially decouple the stock from its weak sector (电池). 30万吨 over 3 years at agreed volumes is rare visibility. But sector gravity is real — sizing down is the right compromise.

5. **Market rhythm: yesterday's -1.7% created entry opportunities.** Multiple candidates (思瑞浦, 上海新阳, 兴森科技) pulled back to MA5/MA10 support during the sell-off and are now bouncing with the sector. This is the ideal entry pattern: buy strength that temporarily dipped, not strength that's spiking.
