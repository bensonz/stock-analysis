## 6. Learnings

1. **`[h028 confirmed]`** Today again validates that tech hardware (通信设备, 元件) leads while cyclicals/defensives lag. The pattern from h028 persists.

2. **`[h027 confirmed]`** Rule 2b (MA-distance) did real work today: 中船特气 (dist_ma5 13.9%), 西部矿业 (dist_ma20 13.3%), 中远海特 (dist_ma20 12.8%) were correctly blocked despite strong fundamentals. Without this rule, V1 would have chased into mean-reversion risk.

3. **`[h013 confirmed]`** Strong breadth alone triggered entry regime `strong`, but I'm still being selective — only 3 positions out of 75 candidates. The candiate-level MA-distance and sector-alignment filters prevent FOMO entries.

4. **New observation:** When IV Rank is extreme (84%+ overall) AND breadth is euphoric (5:1, 0跌停), this is a capitulation bounce. The last time we saw similar readings was during the 03-03 panic week. The lesson from that week: be aggressive on entry but keep stops tight. The 0-risk, high-score names (三环集团, 药明康德) are the best vehicles.

5. **New observation:** The "元件" sector (#3 today, +7.66%) is benefiting from a trifecta: (a) MLCC涨价 from Samsung/太阳诱电, (b) AI server PCB demand from Vera Rubin ramp, (c) 电子布价格五连涨. This is not a one-day spike — it's a sustained supply/demand imbalance thesis. Multiple subsectors converging = stronger sector call.

6. **Data quality flag:** At least 6 stocks (紫光股份, 冰轮环境, 中国巨石, 中钨高新, 桂冠电力, 华工科技, 浪潮信息, 京东方A) show MA distances of -80% to -96%. This points to stock splits or major corporate actions that haven't been adjusted in the MA calculation. These stocks should be treated as "MA data unreliable" and excluded from MA-based decisions.
