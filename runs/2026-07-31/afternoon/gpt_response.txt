## 7. Uncertainty Flags

1. **MA data integrity for ~15 stocks is broken.** I have skipped all of them rather than guessing. This reduces the candidate pool significantly. If these MAs are wrong due to a known corporate action, the enrichment pipeline should be fixed — I'm potentially missing good setups in 自动化设备 and IT服务 (today's hottest sectors).

2. **Sector ranking granularity is coarse.** I only have top 5 and bottom 5 sectors. I'm inferring that 半导体, 元件, 医疗服务 are in "top 30%" based on today's tech rally breadth. But I can't verify with hard data. If any of these sectors are actually in the 30-50% range, my sector-based entry rationale is weaker than it appears.

3. **IV at 81.6% average is genuinely extreme.** The "极度恐慌" signal says "historically medium-term buying opportunity." But the last time we saw comparable levels (7/17-7/22), the market had a V-shaped panic and recovery. If this time is different — if vol stays elevated for weeks — the sizing framework that assumes "wide stops" will produce larger drawdowns. I'm limiting new positions to 9% total (5%+4%) rather than going to the full 15-20% the breadth alone would justify.
