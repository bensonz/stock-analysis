## 7. Uncertainty Flags

- **I'm uncertain whether selling 凯莱英 into a -7.35% market day is the right call.** The stock is outperforming by 4pp. In a normal drawdown, I'd raise the stop to breakeven rather than sell outright. But Rule 5 is explicit: -3% in 3 days = SELL, no exceptions. The PM should weigh whether the -3% rule was designed for normal market conditions and whether a panic overlay exception is warranted.

- **The MA data corruption for ~6 stocks is concerning.** 国瓷材料, 中材科技, 蔚蓝锂芯, 星网锐捷, 顺络电子, 中远海特 all show current prices wildly different from MAs. This suggests the MA data source didn't adjust for corporate actions (splits, bonuses). These stocks should not appear in new position recommendations until this is fixed.

- **IV is at extreme levels; this could mark a capitulation bottom.** Historically, avg IV rank >75% has been a contrarian buy signal for medium-term horizons. If the system had a "panic buying" rule (scaling into the strongest relative-strength names during capitulation), 华峰测控 and 冰轮环境 would be candidates. But V2 explicitly defaults to cash in panic — I'm flagging this as a potential future refinement.
