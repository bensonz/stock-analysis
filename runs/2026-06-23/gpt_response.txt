## 6. Learnings

1. **Macro regime shifts are sector-first events.** The Iran deal didn't just hit oil — it cascaded: oil ↓ → metals ↓ → resources ↓ → rotation into defensives. All 4 冷 sectors are resource/metal, all 5 热 sectors are defensive. This is a clean regime signal. A portfolio 100% in tech/manufacturing has zero exposure to today's leadership. Future construction should ensure at least 1-2 defensive positions when commodity tensions are elevated.

2. **Volume confirmation on distribution days is reliable.** 兴森科技's -9.01% on 8.8x normal volume is a textbook distribution candle. When a stock that's up +34% prints this pattern, it's not a buying opportunity — it's an exit signal. The stop_proximity rule combined with volume analysis saved us from holding through further declines.

3. **The entry_regime hard block is doing its job.** On a day where every enriched candidate looks "almost good enough," the automatic block prevents the exact mistake V1 made: forcing entries in weak conditions. The rule `new_positions: []` is not cowardice — it's discipline.

4. **Sector gravity for 元件 materialized fast.** Just 6 days ago (6/17), 元件 was the #1 sector (+4%). Today it's bottom 5 (-6.34%). This is why Rule 5 exists: "If a position's sector goes cold, SELL regardless." The AI hardware theme hasn't died, but capital is rotating out NOW. We can revisit when the sector stabilizes.

The prudent action is to sell 兴森科技 now and reassess in 3-5 days — but there's a non-trivial chance this is an overreaction that reverses within days. The V1赤峰黄金 lesson (sold at time stop, missed +10% 3 days later) haunts me. However, the stop proximity at 1.63% makes the decision clear: the mechanical stop will trigger either way, so proactive selling is damage control.

2. **I'm uncertain about 路维光电's near-term trajectory.** The -6.34% pullback today is the largest single-day drop since entry. With only 3.74% to breakeven stop, the position could trigger tomorrow. The fundamentals (G11掩膜版, 厦门20亿) haven't changed, but the tape is hostile to semiconductor names. I'm conflicted between "thesis intact → hold" and "price is truth → tight stop is approaching."

3. **The IV data gap is significant but not actionable today.** With hard block in place, even perfect IV data wouldn't change the new positions decision. But for HOLD decisions, not knowing whether 兴森科技 has elevated IV (which would amplify the sell signal) is a missing piece.

4. **The breadth data is misleading.** 2764 up vs 2644 down looks balanced, but the headline indices are deeply red. This suggests massive divergence — large caps (especially cyclicals) are getting crushed while small caps barely rise. This type of breadth often precedes broader weakness as small-cap strength fails to sustain.
