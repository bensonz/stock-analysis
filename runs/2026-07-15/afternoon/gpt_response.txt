But I'm uncertain enough to want 2-3 more days of confirmation before deploying capital even when the regime clears.

2. **"Will the 长鑫科技 IPO on July 16 cause further tech liquidation?"** — The 295B RMB IPO is massive. It could draw liquidity away from existing semiconductor positions, exacerbating the tech decline. Or it could catalyze a半导体 rebound as the "sell the rumor, buy the news" dynamic plays out. I have no conviction either way — which is another reason to stay out.

3. **"Is this the end of the tech bull or a healthy correction?"** — 科创50 was +26% in June alone. A 10-15% correction is normal after that. The Korea/Japan correlation suggests this is global tech profit-taking, not China-specific. But the speed (7.7% single-day crash) and breadth (semiconductor, optical, materials all hit) suggests institutional distribution not just rotation. I'd want to see semis stabilize for 3+ days before re-entering.
