## 6. Learnings

1. **[CRITICAL] The "2/3 indices green" buy gate just saved the portfolio from further destruction.** Breadth was 2.08:1 — deceptively bullish — but all three indices were red. Without this gate, I would have deployed capital into healthcare names that looked good on paper. The gate correctly identified that today was NOT a day to add risk. **This rule is a keeper.**

2. **[CRITICAL] Sector concentration was the silent killer.** All 4 positions opened yesterday were in semiconductor/electronic sectors. When tech rotated out (科创50 -3.67%), there was zero diversification benefit. Going forward: max 50% of positions in any single sector family.

3. **[VALIDATION] IV Rank 100% on 科创50 was a screaming warning.** Yesterday's entries were opened at what was already extreme IV (科创50 IV Rank ~100%). Per V2 IV rules: "Only buy the strongest setups. Wide stops." Yet all 4 positions were opened with standard sizing and tight stops. The vol expansion crushed them within 1 day. **IV rules must be enforced more strictly.**

4. **[PAINFUL BUT CORRECT] -5% stop-loss rule works as designed.** In V1, we would still be holding these positions, rationalizing "thesis still valid." V2's mechanical stop-loss cleanly exits all 4. Yes, it hurts to take -6% to -9% in one day, but the alternative (holding into a multi-week sector rotation) would be far worse. The -3% in first 3 days sub-rule correctly identified bad-timing entries.

5. **[OBSERVATION] Sector rotation from tech to healthcare has been building for 2 weeks.** 科创50 crashed -7.7% on 7/2, bounced weakly, and is now -3.67% again. Meanwhile healthcare has been steadily climbing. The trend was visible but I entered tech positions anyway on 7/14 — exactly when the rotation was confirming. **When a sector-leading index like 科创50 breaks down twice, don't buy dips in that sector.**

6. **[NEW RULE CANDIDATE] Sector family exposure cap: ≤50% of deployed capital in any single sector family (e.g., all semiconductor + electronic chemicals + optical = one family).** Today's 100% tech exposure → 100% drawdown. This is basic portfolio construction that momentum frameworks often neglect.
