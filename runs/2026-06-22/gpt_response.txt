## 6. Key Learnings

1. **Index illusion is real and dangerous**: Today 上证+0.18%/深证+0.29%/创业板+0.74% all green, but breadth is 0.29:1 with 72 stocks at limit-down. This is exactly the type of tape where momentum frameworks must override index-level signals. The `entry_regime` hard block saved us from opening positions into a rout.

2. **The +10% → breakeven stop rule doing its job**: 恒铭达 was at +11.7% on 6/19, stop raised to breakeven. Today -4.48%, now just 3.35% above that stop. Without the mechanical stop-raise, we'd still be at the original -5% entry stop (¥79.37), a much riskier position. The +10% rule converts winners into low-risk holds. [Evidence supporting h023]

3. **新宙邦's 宁德时代 catalyst continues to compound**: The 30万吨/3年 deal has driven the stock from ¥74.65 to ¥88.98 (+19.2%) in 10 days. This reinforces that **contract-based revenue visibility** (not earnings surprise, not concept) is the strongest catalyst type in this market. The stock is approaching +20%, which would trigger a second mechanical stop-raise.

4. **Weak-tape days expose overextended holdings**: 新宙邦's MA distances (dist_ma10=15.8%, dist_ma20=22.2%) are well above chase limits. On a broad selloff day like today, these extended stocks are first to get sold. That 新宙邦 rallied +6.83% anyway speaks to the exceptional catalyst quality — but the extension risk is real and should inform exit timing, not entry.

5. **f10=72 in a "green index" day is a structural warning**: When 72 stocks hit limit-down but indices are green, it means the market is bifurcating into "haves" (AI/semiconductor large caps) and "have-nots" (everything else). This narrow leadership is unsustainable long-term. Either the laggards catch up (broadening) or the leaders get dragged down (mean reversion). Position accordingly — tight stops, no new risk.
