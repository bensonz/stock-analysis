## 6. Learnings

1. **Sector-first discipline is the hardest rule to execute.** With breadth at 18:1, it's tempting to buy any strong stock. But our 4 positions are all in mid-tier sectors, and the #2 sector (元件) has a pristine candidate (广合科技) we almost missed. V2 correctly forces sector-first thinking — but I need to be more aggressive about it.

2. **MA data corruption is a real problem.** Multiple stocks (国瓷材料, 蔚蓝锂芯, 星网锐捷, 中远海特, 顺络电子, 融捷股份) show MA distances of -60% to -95%. These are clearly from stock splits, rights issues, or data pipeline errors. This creates a blind spot — we can't apply Rule 2b to these names, so we must skip them rather than guess.

3. **Stop proximity is the #1 risk for today's positions.** We opened 4 positions and 3 have stop-proximity flags (1 WARNING, 2 WATCH). The entry precision needs work — we're buying after the rally has already started, which compresses the stop cushion. Tomorrow's open will be critical.

4. **18:1 breadth days are rare and should be exploited.** This is the most bullish breadth reading I've seen. Cash at 79% is too conservative. The rules say max 8 positions and we have 4 — adding 1 more in the strongest sector is the right move.
