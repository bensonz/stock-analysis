## 6. Learnings

1. **Breadth gate works**: Today is a textbook case — indices are green (上证 +0.5%, 深证 +0.61%), but 3,455 stocks are declining vs 1,923 advancing. Without the breadth gate, a naive system would see "market up, let's buy." V2 correctly stays out. This is related to **h013** (strong breadth alone is not enough — and the inverse: weak breadth means stay out even when indices look OK).

2. **IV 100% is not a buy signal by itself**: The options market pricing extreme tail risk is consistent with a post-crash fragile tape. History says these are good medium-term entry zones, but V2's job is to wait for breadth to confirm the turn before deploying capital. Related: **h017** (low IV doesn't justify freezing; high IV doesn't justify forcing).

3. **株冶集团 is exactly the kind of stock V1 would have missed**: PE 13, "low quality" dividend score, industrial metals sector that V1 might have dismissed as cyclical. But it's in the #2 sector today with RPS in the sweet-extended zone and reasonable MA distances. This validates V2's philosophy shift.

4. **Most candidates are in drawdown, not setup**: 90%+ of the strategy pool has negative MA distances. This is not a "buy the dip" list — it's a "wait for stabilization" list. Only 株冶集团 and 横店东磁 are within 5% of any short-term MA.

5. **The "missed opportunities" from yesterday's WATCH list reinforce V2**: 扬杰科技 was WATCH yesterday at ¥102, today at ¥93.07 (-8.8%). 星宸科技 WATCH at ¥145.1, today at ¥91.85 (-36.7% — massive). V1's WATCH conservatism was accidentally correct in a crashing tape, but for the wrong reasons. V2's approach of "buy small or skip" would have avoided these traps.
