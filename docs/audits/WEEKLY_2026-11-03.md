## Issues Found

1. **Exits, not entries, lose the money.** This is the same finding as 08-16, and it is still open.
2. **Skips that cite margin deleveraging** are the only skip reason that costs money (n=23, mean would-be +1.39%, 7 of 16 missed wins). This goes against ANALYST.md:191, which says not to reject a clean setup on margin alone.
3. **The prompt carries a stale re-entry statistic.** `run_daily.py:1627` prints "重入 n=9 均值 −3.71%", a pre-epoch number. The current numbers are n=7, −1.86%. Also, the 14-day 近期已平仓 window missed both re-entries after the fix.
4. **The sector rules cannot be checked.** The prompt has only a one-day SW L2 top5/bottom5. On a 5-day L1 proxy, 19 of 34 entries were in bottom-30% sectors. A strict 3-day cold exit would have cut 2 of the 3 biggest winners.
5. **Event placeholders raised `risk_window`.** 11 of 17 sessions since 10-12 were `event_imminent`. AI `high` keeps were the only cause on just 2 of them. Stale placeholders (五中全会 10-20, 政治局 10-30) caused others.
6. **Data hygiene is clean today.** `factors verify` OK, no partial days. The afternoon spot check verified nothing for 39 days. **Fixed today** (`pricedb/health.py`).
7. **Rule compliance: 0 positions** held past the −5% stop or the 15-day time stop. A pipeline outage hid one −3% early breach on 09-07 (601872); no run saw it.
